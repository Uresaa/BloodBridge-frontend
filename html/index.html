How BloodBridge Works

A simple, fast, and secure process that connects blood donors with patients in need during emergencies.

01

Submit a Request

Hospitals or patients create an emergency blood request by selecting the blood type, location, and urgency level.

02

Smart Matching

BloodBridge instantly finds nearby donors with the required blood type and sends them real-time notifications.

03

Save a Life

The donor accepts the request, visits the medical center, and completes the donation to help save someone's life.
