Terms and Conditions

These Terms and Conditions govern access to and use of BloodBridge, a platform that helps connect blood donors with patients and hospitals. By creating an account or using BloodBridge, you agree to these terms.

1. Purpose of the service

BloodBridge is a coordination tool. It is not a hospital, blood bank, medical provider, or emergency service. Matching a donor with a request does not guarantee that blood will be available, compatible, or delivered in time.

2. Eligibility

You must provide accurate information and be legally able to enter into this agreement. Donors are responsible for confirming their own eligibility to donate according to applicable health rules and the receiving facility.

3. Accounts

You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential and for activity on your account. Notify us at support@bloodbridge.com if you believe your account has been used without permission.

4. Donor and requester responsibilities

  • Requests must be truthful about blood type, urgency, location, and hospital or patient details.
  • Donors must not offer blood if they know they are ineligible or if doing so would put anyone at risk.
  • Clinical screening, testing, and transfusion remain the responsibility of licensed medical staff.

5. Location and personal data

BloodBridge may store account details, blood type, role, and optional location so that nearby requests and donors can be matched. We use this information to operate the service, send notifications you enable, and keep the platform secure. Do not post sensitive medical records that are not needed to complete a request.

6. Acceptable use

You may not misuse BloodBridge, including by creating fake requests, impersonating others, scraping data, interfering with other users, or using the service for any unlawful purpose.

7. No medical advice

Content on BloodBridge is for coordination only and is not medical advice. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

8. Limitation of liability
